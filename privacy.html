Shardtown Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Shardtown is a free browser game made by WolfAtTheGate LLC ("we", "us"). We don't have accounts, we don't ask for your name or email, and we don't sell your data. This page explains the little information that is collected when you play, and why.

Google Analytics

Google Analytics only runs if you choose "Allow" when the game asks. If you choose "No thanks", it never loads and no analytics cookies are set. The game works the same either way.

When it runs, Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and sends usage data to Google. Google uses your IP address to estimate your location and does not show it to us. We never send Google anything that identifies you personally. You can read how Google uses this data.

Multiplayer

Multiplayer is peer-to-peer: your game talks directly to the other players' games, not to a server of ours. To find each other, games use the public Nostr relay network and public STUN servers. Because of this, the other players in your room and those relay and STUN operators can see your IP address. The only gameplay data shared is what the game needs to show you to others, such as your position, vehicle and actions. Only join rooms with people you're comfortable sharing that with. Multiplayer is off unless you create or join a room, which includes opening someone's room invite link.

Hosting

The game is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub keeps logs of visits, including IP addresses, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Children

Shardtown is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices and rights

Because we don't have accounts and Google Analytics doesn't show us who you are, we usually can't connect any data to you. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the use of your data. To ask about any of this, contact us below and we'll help as far as we can. Clearing your browser's cookies and site data removes everything stored on your device.

Changes

If the game starts collecting anything new, for example if we add accounts or ads, we'll update this page and the date at the top before that change goes live.
